Get Checky on your phone

Two homes, one plan. Takes about 3 minutes.

  1. 1

    Open www.checkyapp.co.za

    On iPhone use Safari, on Android use Chrome. Type the address at the top and open it.

Seeing "Unsafe app blocked" from Google Play Protect? That happens when Checky is installed from Samsung Internet or from a link inside WhatsApp, Facebook or email. Checky is safe; it is just the website on your home screen. Tap OK, then open the Chrome app itself, type www.checkyapp.co.za and do steps 2 to 4 again. Chrome installs it without the warning.
Stuck? Make sure you confirmed your email (step 3), then sign in again. Still not working? Send a WhatsApp or email to checkdieapp@gmail.com.
